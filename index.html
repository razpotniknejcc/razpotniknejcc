strawberrycheesecake
Cottage cheese · no added sugar
5 minprep
24 hfreeze
165kcal / pint
21 gprotein
14 gcarbs
4 gfat
Ingredients
150 glow-fat cottage cheese
200 mlalmond milk
80 gstrawberries
1 tbsperythritol
½ tspvanilla
1wholegrain biscuit
Method
  1. Blend the cottage cheese, milk, strawberries, erythritol and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Pour into the pint up to the MAX line and freeze for 24 hours on a flat surface.
  3. Run the LITE ICE CREAM program. If it's crumbly, add 1 tbsp milk and select RE-SPIN.
  4. Make a well, add the crushed biscuit and select MIX-IN.
Tip: leave the pint on the counter for 10 minutes before spinning and you'll almost never need a re-spin.
